My name is Levi Gravely, and I am a Computer Science major with a concentration in Information Security. Throughout my academic program, I have developed a stronger foundation in problem-solving, software development, project planning, and security-focused thinking. One of the most important skills I strengthened was the ability to solve technical problems efficiently through testing, iteration, and careful analysis. Rather than approaching code as something that should work on the first attempt, I learned to evaluate errors, run multiple trials, and refine my solutions until they met the intended requirements.
---
This program also helped me understand the value of thoughtful planning and professional time management. In both academic and corporate environments, deadlines are important, but quality and accuracy are equally essential. I learned to begin planning early, identify limitations, account for potential risks, and create realistic timelines for completing work. Developing my ePortfolio gave me the opportunity to apply these habits while also strengthening my coding skills and exploring enhancements that challenged me to think beyond my original design.
---
A major area of growth for me was learning how agile practices support collaboration and project delivery. Concepts such as sprints, project boards, and structured communication helped me connect classroom learning to my professional experience. In my current role, I have participated in collaborative efforts involving load balancing, DNS work, and emergency break-fix meetings. Understanding agile terminology helped me recognize that many of these practices were already part of my work environment. Tools such as Azure DevOps boards support transparency by helping teams track progress, communicate responsibilities, and respond quickly to changing needs.
---
My ePortfolio enhancements demonstrate my growth across user experience, software design, database performance, and security. For my first enhancement, I added a QR scanner to an inventory application. This feature improved usability by allowing users to scan inventory items instead of entering them manually. Completing this enhancement encouraged me to think more creatively about how to interpret customer needs and translate broad requirements into practical technical solutions. This skill is especially valuable in professional environments, where clients may know what outcome they want but may not know how that outcome should be implemented.
For my second enhancement, I focused on improving the efficiency and structure of the application. The original program did not manage data as efficiently as it could, so I implemented database indexing to improve performance. By using indexes, the application no longer needed to scan the entire database for each request; instead, it could locate and retrieve only the necessary data. This improvement reduced unnecessary processing and aligned with one of my core professional values: building efficient and reliable systems. I also improved the project structure by supporting persistent data storage and updating the application to use a version catalog in TOML, which reflects current best practices in newer versions of Android Studio.
For my third enhancement, I focused on security and access control. The original application allowed users to gain full access after account creation, regardless of whether they were standard users or administrators. To address this issue, I added login controls, stored authentication information in the database, and implemented password protection. I also updated permission levels so that standard users have read-only access while administrators retain full permissions. This enhancement directly supports my professional goal of moving further into information security because it allowed me to practice database security, authentication, and role-based access control.
----
In addition to the technical work, this program strengthened my understanding of cybersecurity concepts such as CVEs, patching, and vulnerability management. As artificial intelligence and other emerging technologies continue to influence the software landscape, organizations must patch systems more frequently and stay aware of vulnerabilities that could be exploited. Learning how to research CVEs and understand their impact has moved me closer to my goal of becoming a Cybersecurity Analyst. These experiences helped me connect software development, network engineering, and security into a broader professional skill set.
Together, these enhancements represent my ability to design solutions that are usable, efficient, and secure. The QR scanner improved the user experience, the indexing and project updates improved application performance and maintainability, and the login and permission changes strengthened the application’s security posture. Each enhancement reflects a different part of my development as a computer science professional. As I continue building my career, I will carry forward the critical thinking skills, planning strategies, technical knowledge, and security mindset that I strengthened throughout this capstone experience.
